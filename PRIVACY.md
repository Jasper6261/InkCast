# InkCast（墨投）隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 18 日

## 中文

**我们收集什么：不收集。** InkCast 不需要注册账号，不连接我们的服务器，也不包含广告或统计分析组件。

**数据在哪里：**
- **笔迹和电脑屏幕画面：**只在你的电脑和你的 iPad 之间、通过你的局域网传输，不会上传到互联网。
- **保存的文件：**截图、白板存档、画笔设置、配对信息，只保存在你自己的电脑上。
  - 安装版：保存在系统的用户数据目录；截图保存在“图片 / InkCast”。
  - 免安装版：保存在程序所在的文件夹。
- **画笔设置和语言选择：**保存在 iPad 浏览器的本地存储里。
- **书写诊断数据：**只在你点击“发送书写诊断到电脑”时生成，保存在你的电脑上；不会发给我们，除非你自己发送。
- **崩溃日志：**只保存在你的电脑上，内容是程序版本和出错位置，不含笔迹或屏幕内容。

**屏幕画面：**“向 iPad 发送屏幕画面”默认开启，可以随时在设置里关闭。关闭后，电脑不再截取屏幕。

**权限：**
- **Windows：**可能需要允许 InkCast 通过防火墙，以便 iPad 在局域网内连接。
- **Mac：**需要“屏幕录制”权限（用于把电脑画面发送到 iPad）和局域网访问权限。

**儿童：**本软件不面向 13 岁以下儿童收集任何信息（本软件不收集任何人的信息）。

**联系我们：**在本项目的 GitHub 页面提交 Issue；安全问题请按 `SECURITY.md` 私下报告。

## English

**What we collect: nothing.** InkCast needs no account, does not connect to our servers, and contains no ads or analytics.

**Where your data stays:**
- **Strokes and screen images:** travel only between your computer and your iPad over your local network. They are never uploaded to the internet.
- **Saved files:** screenshots, whiteboard files, brush settings and pairing information are stored only on your own computer.
  - Installed version: in the system's per-user app data folder; screenshots go to Pictures / InkCast.
  - Portable version: next to the app.
- **Brush settings and language choice:** stored in the iPad browser's local storage.
- **Writing diagnostics:** created only when you tap "Send writing diagnostics to PC" and saved on your computer. They are not sent to us unless you send them yourself.
- **Crash logs:** stay on your computer. They contain the app version and the error location, never strokes or screen content.

**Screen images:** "Send screen to iPad" is on by default and can be turned off in Settings at any time. When it is off, the computer stops capturing the screen.

**Permissions:**
- **Windows:** you may need to allow InkCast through the firewall so the iPad can connect over your local network.
- **Mac:** InkCast needs Screen Recording permission (to send your screen to the iPad) and local network access.

**Contact:** open an issue on the project’s GitHub page; report security problems privately as described in `SECURITY.md`.
